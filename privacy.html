Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a plain-language starting policy for a small, early-stage business. It is not a substitute for legal advice — before you rely on it, especially if you'll handle clients' personal or financial data at scale, have a lawyer review it against the laws that apply where your clients live (for example state privacy laws in the US, or GDPR if you have EU visitors).

What we collect

When you fill out the trial or contact form on this site, we collect the information you choose to submit: your name, email address, phone number (if provided), agency or brokerage name, and any listing or business details you share with us.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, deliver the content or trial you requested, and communicate with you about your account. We do not sell your information to third parties.

How it's processed

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox using FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service. We do not run our own database or server for this site — it is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your browser's IP address as part of loading those files.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information in our email inbox for as long as needed to serve you as a client, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing trevqioagency@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to trevqioagency@gmail.com.
